# Scion Asset Management (Michael Burry) — research log

Written 2026-11-19 by a helper session. Period: twelve 13F quarters, Q4 2022 (period 2022-12-31) through Q3 2025 (2025-09-30), then Burry's public writing to 2026-11-19.

## Read this first: what is odd about this manager's filings

- **No 13F after 2025-11-03.** Scion's SEC adviser registration was terminated effective 2025-11-10 (IAPD; Reuters, Bloomberg). The last 13F (period 2025-09-30) was filed 2025-11-03, a week after the 2025-10-27 investor letter announcing liquidation. Nothing has been filed under Scion's CIK 1649339 or its two included managers (CIK 1768021 Scion Asset Partners LP, CIK 1768023 Scion Capital Group LLC) since then; confirmed both from the archive folder listings and from EDGAR full-text search (zero Scion/Burry filings after 2025-11-04). No 13F for 2025-12-31 exists. Whether one was owed depends on whether Scion still had discretion over 13(f) securities at year-end after liquidating; I could not determine that and would not read anything into its absence.
- **All twelve quarters reconcile exactly** with their cover-page entry count and value total (whole dollars). All rows are `SH`; there are no `PRN` rows.
- **Q4 2023 amendment.** The original 13F-HR (0000905148-24-000653, filed 2024-02-14) reported the summary-page entry count as 0. A 13F-HR/A RESTATEMENT (0000905148-24-000751, filed 2024-02-16) says it was filed "solely to correct the 'Form 13F Information Table Entry Total' ... inadvertently reported as '0' ... due to a software error". Same 25 rows, same total $94,591,240. I used the original's table with the amendment's count.
- **Options dominate the reported totals.** Six quarters have option rows (Q2 2023, Q3 2023, Q3 2024, Q1 2025, Q2 2025, Q3 2025); in all but Q3 2024 the option notional is more than half the reported total (Q2 2023 94%, Q3 2023 56%, Q1 2025 93%, Q2 2025 90%, Q3 2025 95%). Option rows carry the value of the underlying shares, not the premium. So `pct_of_portfolio` in the CSVs (row value / 13F reported total) is badly distorted in those quarters: e.g. Palantir puts show as 66% of Q3 2025, but Burry said the premium was $9.2M in a fund with roughly $155M of regulatory AUM. Option rows show 0 in the voting-authority columns, stock rows show sole authority.
- **Cover-page explanatory notes** (Item "additional information" = Y) appear in Q2 2023, Q3 2023, Q3 2024 and Q1 2025, each stating that long puts are reported at the underlying's value. Q2/Q3 2023 add that "the Form 13F report excludes any short positions ... which may serve to hedge the long positions". Q1 2025 says the puts "may serve to hedge long positions which are not eligible to be reported" under Rule 13f-1(c). Q2 2025 and Q3 2025 have options but no note.
- **"Other included managers" toggles between 0 and 2** (2 in Q1 2023, Q2 2023, Q1 2024, Q3 2024, Q1–Q3 2025; 0 otherwise). In the "2" quarters, the two affiliates filed 13F-NT notices pointing to Scion. The toggle does not change what is reported; I treat it as clerical.
- **Filing agent changed**: accession prefixes 0000905148 (2023–Nov 2024), 0001879202 (Feb–Aug 2025), then Scion's own 0001649339 for the last filing. Not meaningful.
- **Same issuer, two rows**: stock and an option on the same name are kept as separate positions in the CSVs (keyed on CUSIP + put/call). Bruker appears in Q3 2025 as a 6.375% Series A preferred (CUSIP 116794207), a different security from the common held in earlier quarters.

## 2026-11-19: holdings rebuilt from SEC filings

The holdings CSVs were regenerated by `scripts/build13f.py` from the information-table XML fetched directly from sec.gov, and compared row by row (CUSIP plus put/call) with the hand-built CSVs. Shares and values were identical in every quarter, so no transcription error was found. Differences are cosmetic: issuer and class text is now as filed rather than normalised by hand, and `pct_of_portfolio` has four decimals. Each holdings folder now has `sources.csv`, listing the accessions behind each quarter and how amendments were applied. Filer errors that were never amended are fixed in `managers.yaml` (`corrections`, `accepted_gaps`) rather than by hand.
