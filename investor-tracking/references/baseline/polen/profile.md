**Manager:** Polen Capital Management, LLC (Boca Raton, FL). CIK 0001034524, 13F file no. 028-03687, CRD 106093, SEC file 801-15180.
**People who matter for the 13F:** Dan Davidowitz and Damon Ficklin (co-PMs, Focus Growth / large-company growth; with CEO Stan Moss they control the firm). Drew Cupps (5Perspectives Growth team, joined June 30, 2025). The 13F is signed by the CCO: Brian D. Goldberg, then Lauren Walsh (from the May 2024 filings), then Goldberg again as General Counsel and CCO (Feb 2026), then Richard J. Simek (Q3 2026 filing).
**Period covered:** 13F-HR for Q4 2023 through Q3 2026 (12 quarters, all reconciled to the cover page), plus the other SEC filings for the same window.
**Written:** 2026-11-19. Holdings CSVs are in `holdings/`. Every filing and source is listed in `log.md`.

Throughout, the three kinds of statement are labelled. **Said** is Polen's own words, with a link. **Filed** is what the 13F or another SEC filing shows. **Inferred** is my reading, with a confidence level and what would change my mind.

## The short version

Read a Polen 13F as one concentrated portfolio wrapped inside a shrinking firm. About 25 rows worth $100M or more make up Focus Growth, the large-cap quality-growth strategy run by Davidowitz and Ficklin. Through 2025 those rows were 96–98% of reported value, and they were still about 90% in Q3 2026. Everything else is several smaller books: international and global growth, emerging markets, a small-cap team that arrived mid-2025, and some ETF and credit odds and ends. They add up to a long list of rows but very little money.

The most important fact about this window is that **most quarter-to-quarter share changes are client redemptions, not decisions**. The 13F total fell from $41.5B (Q4 2023) to $9.2B (Q3 2026). From Q3 2025 on, the median core position lost 20–27% of its shares every quarter. To see what the managers actually decided, compare each name's share change with that quarter's median. Then check it against the quarterly Focus Growth commentary, which names every initiation and exit and usually gives the reason.

The story underneath the numbers is a quality-growth shop being forced to change its mind about AI. Polen avoided semiconductors through mid-2025 and paid for it. In August 2025 it bought NVIDIA and Broadcom. By mid-2026 it was openly saying it would be "more nimble" and had moved into industrial and AI-infrastructure names. Turnover in the core book roughly tripled.

## How to tell the books apart

This is the main skill for reading a Polen 13F. The rules below come from matching every Focus Growth initiation and exit named in the quarterly commentaries against the filings, and from the Polen Growth Fund's January 2026 N-PORT. That N-PORT lists 25 stocks, and every one is a $100M+ row in the Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 13Fs.

**Rule 1: size.** A Focus Growth position is a $100M+ row, usually several hundred million to a few billion dollars. Focus Growth is now only about $8–9B, so its smallest positions (1–2% weights) can sit near $100M; EMCOR entered at $86M in Q2 2026. In practice:
- a new row above about $75M with a matching name in the commentary is Focus Growth;
- a row under about $30M almost never is.

*(Inferred; high confidence through 2025, medium for 2026 as the book shrinks.)*

**Rule 2: what remains after a Focus Growth exit.** When Focus Growth sells a name that another strategy also owns, the 13F row usually does not disappear. It drops to a $10–60M remnant belonging to Global Growth, International Growth or another sleeve. Examples:

| Name | Exited by Focus Growth | 13F row afterwards |
|---|---|---|
| Aon | Q2 2026 | $54M |
| Uber | Q2 2026 | $41M |
| MSCI | Q3 2026 | $32M |
| Accenture | Q2 2026 | $19M |

Gartner and Adobe went straight to under $3M. Thermo Fisher stopped at $56M for one quarter, then fell to $1M. A remnant can itself be sold off a quarter later. Treat a big drop to a small remnant as an exit, and do not read the remnant as conviction.

**Rule 3: the reverse also happens.** A name can sit in the 13F at Global Growth size before Focus Growth buys it. Aon was a $240–280M row through 2024 (Global Growth) before Focus Growth "initiated" it in Q1 2025 and the row jumped to $1.2B. Two more examples: ASML and TSMC were held in Global Growth before Focus Growth bought them in 2026, and Global Growth bought Willis Towers Watson in Q1 2025.

**Rule 4: what lives in the small rows.**

| Small rows | Likely book | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| HDFC Bank, ICICI, Tencent Music, PDD, KB Financial, Kaspi, dLocal, Yum China, Nu, Sea, MercadoLibre, MakeMyTrip, the Argentine ADRs (Galicia, Supervielle, IRSA, Loma Negra, YPF, Vista) | Emerging-markets and international teams; only US-listed ADRs and NY shares can appear | medium-high |
| Globant, ICON, SAP, ADP, Medtronic, Spotify, monday.com, On Holding, ASML before 2026 | Global and International Growth | medium-high |
| AAON, Construction Partners, Core & Main, Goosehead, Hamilton Lane, Progyny, Revolve, Warby Parker, SiteOne and similar, mostly through mid-2025 | US/Global SMID and small-company teams | medium |
| From Q2 2025: microcaps and thematic small caps, such as Aehr, Amprius, AirJoule, Absci, Bloom Energy, BWXT, NuScale, Joby, Archer, TeraWulf, Powell, IES, CECO, plus many biotechs | 5Perspectives (Cupps) team | high |

The 5Perspectives assignment is high confidence because the row count jumped from 117 to 240 in exactly the quarter the team joined (June 30, 2025), and its stated themes are "electrification, AI, genomics, and aerospace" ([InvestmentNews, 2026-05-21](https://www.investmentnews.com/etfs/polen-capital-aims-to-boost-advisor-choice-with-active-growth-equity-etfs/266698)).

**Rule 5: ETFs are plumbing, not views.**
- The iShares Russell 1000 Growth and Vanguard Growth rows are cash equitization. The Polen Growth Fund itself held 70,000 Vanguard Growth ETF shares at 1/31/2026 (N-PORT). The iShares Russell 1000 Growth row was $367M in Q4 2025 and falls to $10M in Q3 2026. When it swells, money is moving between Focus Growth accounts or awaiting redemption.
- The iShares MSCI ACWI and ACWI ex-US rows play the same role for the global books. The Timothy Plan and Global X Catholic ETFs look like faith-based client sleeves.
- iShares iBoxx High Yield (200,000 shares in Q3 2025) and iShares Broad USD High Yield belong to the credit side.
- From Q3 2026 the 13F also holds Polen's own FundVantage ETFs (5Perspectives Small-Mid, 5Perspectives Large and Focus Growth, $5.7M together). That is most likely seed money or client accounts holding the house ETFs.

*(Inferred; medium-high.)*

**Rule 6: only Q4 2025 has options, and they are not Focus Growth.** The only put/call rows in twelve quarters are three PUT rows in Q4 2025: Bloom Energy ($1.9M), BWX Technologies ($2.2M) and SoFi ($1.1M). Each sits beside a long position in the same stock. These are hedges on 5Perspectives names. Focus Growth has never used options in this window. *(Inferred; high.)*

## Flows versus decisions: the working method

The procedure follows from the arc.

1. Take every Focus Growth-sized row present in both quarters.
2. Compute each one's share change, and take the median. That median is the flow for the quarter.
3. A name that moves with the median was simply carried along by flows.
4. A name well above the median was bought on purpose. Well below the median, it was sold on purpose.

Q3 2026 is a good example. The median was −26%:

| Name | Share change | Reading | Commentary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta | +52% | real add | "Added" |
| EMCOR | +36% | real add | "Added" |
| Lilly | −45% | real trim | "Trimmed" |
| Shopify | −47% | real trim | "Trimmed" |
| Starbucks | −53% | real trim | "Trimmed" |
| Microsoft, Visa, Amazon | −21% to −27% | flow only | no mention |

Q2 2026 shows that the commentary's "added" is relative to the flow. NVIDIA was "added" but its share count fell 3%, while everything else fell about 27%.

Check stock splits before computing ratios. ServiceNow's count went 1.12M → 5.68M in Q4 2025, consistent with a 5-for-1 split. Netflix went 478,880 → 173,797 in the same quarter, which after its 10-for-1 split is a near-total sale; that matches the stated exit. In Q2 2026 the iShares Russell 1000 Growth and Vanguard Growth ETF rows show per-share values falling about 3.4x and 5x. That looks like ETF splits, which I did not verify.

## How to read the next filing (Q4 2026, due mid-February 2027)

- **Start with the cover page.** Look at total value and row count. A total well below about $9B means outflows continued. Q2 to Q3 2026 was −21% on value, with the market roughly flat-to-up for these names.
- **Check the signer.** The CCO has changed three times in three years.
- **Read the Focus Growth Q4 2026 commentary first** (usually out about three weeks after quarter-end, on polencapital.com and reprinted by Seeking Alpha). Every Focus Growth initiation and exit will be named there. Then verify each one in the 13F using the rules above.
- **Read add/trim language against the quarter's median.**
- **Track the drift.** The share of value in $100M+ rows fell from about 97% to about 90% in 2026. If it keeps falling, Focus Growth is shrinking faster than the other books, and the size threshold in Rule 1 needs lowering. Expect more industrial, AI-infrastructure and semiconductor names, and expect shorter holding periods.
- **Watch for new small-cap clusters, option rows, or very small ETF positions.** These are almost certainly 5Perspectives activity and say nothing about Focus Growth.
- **Watch for missing rows.** International Equity (the CastleArk team, now a separate majority-team-owned LLC) may file separately or not at all. If international ADR rows disappear, check whether they moved there rather than being sold.

## Where the 13F misleads

- **Q4 2023 values are wrong in Polen's own filing, and stayed wrong through two amendments.** In that filing, groups of rows filed in sequence repeat the value of the row that follows them:
  - $81,021,086 appears on Eli Lilly, Goosehead, Helen of Troy, Illumina, iShares MSCI ACWI, Karooooo and Medtronic;
  - $67,858,652 appears on Hamilton Lane, Houlihan Lokey, two iShares rows, Las Vegas Sands, MarketAxess and MercadoLibre;
  - $5,171,232 appears on Globant, HDFC Bank, two iShares rows, LGI Homes and Medpace.

  In each group only the last row is correct: Medtronic at $82.38, MercadoLibre at $1,571.53 and Medpace at $306.53 match year-end prices. The others inherited those values, and ICON is also wrong ($5.3M for about 1.19M shares). Eli Lilly's 594,801 shares were worth about $347M, not $81M. ICON's were worth about $337M. Corrected, the cover total understates holdings by roughly $0.7–0.8B (under 2%). The rows still sum exactly to the filed total, so this is a filing error, not a parsing error.

  The CSV keeps the values as filed so it reconciles to the cover page. **Do not compute Q4 2023 → Q1 2024 changes in value for those names.** Use shares, which look right. This is why ICON and Globant appear as "new" $100M+ rows in Q1 2024 when they were not new. Amendment 2 (2024-05-10) fixed only ICON's figure and a mislabelled row, where a Forum Energy note was filed under the name "FLOOR & DECOR". Q3 2024 was also restated, by +$269K, which is immaterial.
- **Cover page.** No other included managers in any quarter. No confidential-treatment filings found. Values are in whole dollars in every quarter, including the Q4 2023 filing, so no thousands scaling is needed. 13F totals track ADV discretionary AUM, but they exclude about $9.3B of model/UMA assets (12/31/2025) and anything managed solely by Polen Capital Credit.
- **What cannot appear.** Foreign-listed shares are invisible. Focus Growth's Q3 2026 purchase of Adyen (Amsterdam) has no 13F row. ASML and TSMC appear only because Polen holds the NY shares and ADRs, and the emerging-markets books show only their US-listed ADRs. Focus Growth's non-US weight is therefore understated, and an initiation in a foreign line will look like nothing happened.
- **PRN rows.** The only principal-amount row is the Forum Energy Technologies 9% convertible note (CUSIP 34984VAC4) in Q4 2023 and Q1 2024, about $23.6–24.4M of principal. It belongs to the credit affiliate and was gone by Q2 2024. Polen also filed 13G/As on Forum Energy (January 2024). Read it as a credit holding, not an equity view.
- **Options.** Only the three Q4 2025 puts described in Rule 6. Their value field is the underlying shares' value, not premium.
- **Holdings versus conviction.** Because the 13F total fell 78% in eleven quarters, almost every row shrinks. "Polen cut Microsoft by 60%" across 2025–26 is a redemption story, not a sale decision.

## What would change my mind

- **On the size rule:** if a Focus Growth commentary ever names an initiation that shows up below about $50M, or a $100M+ row that no Focus Growth document mentions (other than ETF plumbing), the strategies overlap more than I think. The likeliest place is Global Growth buying a Focus Growth name at scale.
